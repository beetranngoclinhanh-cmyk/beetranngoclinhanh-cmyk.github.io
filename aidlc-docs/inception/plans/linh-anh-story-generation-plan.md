## Proposed methodology

Use reader journeys to organise a concise set of stories: discover Linh Anh, explore analytical projects, assess research and internships, understand education and awards, discover leadership/community work, access evidence/resume, and contact her. Include mobile/keyboard navigation as a reader outcome.

Use “As a …, I want …, so that …” with observable acceptance criteria, source/requirement references, and persona mappings. Aim for approximately eight small stories; split only where outcomes can be assessed independently. No invented demographic details or new product features.

## Approach options and trade-offs

- Journey-based (recommended): follows discovery, evaluation and contact; best fit for this portfolio.
- Feature-based: easy mapping to page sections but can miss the end-to-end reader experience.
- Persona-based: highlights reader motivations but duplicates shared browsing needs.
- Domain-based: groups analytics, finance and community work but fragments a reader’s overall assessment.
- Epic-based: useful for large products; adds unnecessary hierarchy here.
